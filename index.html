// BODY / ART / ABSURDITY / MATTER / PERFORMANCE // 

Skin, ink, silence. 
Absurd
Nothing decorative.
Nothing illustrative.
Everything that followed came from there.

It did not begin as a plan.
It emerged from an encounter with the absurd.
This project does not seek explanation.
It seeks presence.
Paint, fabric and skin are not supports.
They are matter.
The body does not illustrate an idea.
It becomes the place where the work takes place.

A performance becomes an encounter.
The encounter becomes a trace.
And once the work leaves the body,
its meaning is no longer mine.

This project does not seek explanation.
It seeks presence.

ABSURD. ABSURD SENSIBILITY.

Every answer is true to the one who gives it.
There is no interference. Nothing is definitive.
Everything coexists.

Skin to eyes.
Matter to image.
Body to thought.

What do you see?
What do you feel?

My absurd has no beginning and no end.
It is an ongoing field of interpretation.

The absurd is my revolt armed with art, paint and skin.
